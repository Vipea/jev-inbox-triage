Post text

I gave 60 customer emails to an AI that can't write a single word 😂

It sorted all of them. For less than one cent in total.

It's called Jev, and it's a new type of AI called a decision model. It doesn't chat or write replies. You ask it something like "which team should handle this email?" or "is this customer about to leave?" and it gives back an answer plus how sure it is. Honestly, that's all a lot of businesses need.

So I tested it on a pretty realistic support inbox:

93% of emails went to the right team
about a third of a second per email
all 8 customers who were quietly about to leave got flagged, even though none of them wrote "cancel"

The part I found most interesting is that it knows when it's guessing. When it was less than 60% sure, it was wrong 3 out of 4 times. When it was more sure than that, it was right 55 out of 56 times.

So you don't have to automate everything. Let it handle the 93% it's confident about and send the rest to a real person. That's how we build these systems for clients anyway, and it's nice to see the numbers back it up.

Code and all 60 emails: github.com/Vipea/jev-inbox-triage if you want to try it on your own inbox.
